Longhand

Give your thoughts a place.

A private journal for Android. Your writing stays on your phone, in an encrypted database, behind a passcode you set.

What it does

  • Write daily, with titles, tags, photographs and voice notes.
  • Keep a focus: something you are working through, with its own timeline and reminders.
  • See what you have written about, from patterns found on the device.
  • Back up to your own Google Drive, encrypted so that nobody else can read it.

Where your writing lives

On your phone. The database is encrypted on disk, and the key is held in your device's hardware keystore. There is no Longhand account and no Longhand server — nothing is uploaded unless you ask for it.

If you turn on backup, the file goes to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only this app can see, and it is sealed with your passcode before it leaves the phone. Google stores the file; Google cannot read it. Nor can anyone else, including me, without that passcode.

Privacy

No analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no third-party SDKs collecting anything. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
